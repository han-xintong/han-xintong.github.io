About Xintong Han

Assistant Professor of Marketing · Université Laval

Department of Marketing, Faculty of Business Administration. University profile

Research interests

  • 01

    Digital platforms

    How commission rules, badges, recommendation systems, and public platforms reshape participation, pricing, and quality.

  • 02

    Networks

    How social learning, peer effects, and technological dependencies transmit information and adoption decisions across connected markets.

  • 03

    Pricing & welfare

    How multidimensional price schedules change consumer demand, firm strategy, market allocation, and policy trade-offs.

  • 04

    Empirical methods

    Structural econometrics, causal inference, and machine learning in econometrics using experiments, panel designs, instruments, and dynamic models.
