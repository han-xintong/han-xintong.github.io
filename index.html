The effects of online referrals on subscription renewals: Evidence from a digital content platform

Manufacturing & Service Operations Management · forthcoming

Xintong Han, Shiliang Cui, Xin (Shane) Wang, Shaojia Wang & Tong Wang

On a Chinese digital content platform, users renew more often when their referrer renews, yet referred-in users renew less often than non-referred users. At the same expected discount cost, the model predicts more renewals when discounts go to referred-in customers than when they go to referrers.

Digital revitalization or useless effort? Public e-commerce support and local specialty sales

Journal of Development Economics · 2026

Xintong Han, Jan Victor Dee, Shaojia Wang & Kefan Chen

Access to a government e-commerce platform raised Pu’er tea farmers’ online sales by about 17% and cut their offline sales by about 16%. Sales moved online rather than grew.

Effectiveness of online education during the COVID-19 pandemic: Evidence from Chinese universities

Production and Operations Management · 2026

Xintong Han, Shijie Lu, Xin (Shane) Wang & Nan Cui

When nine Chinese universities moved teaching online during the COVID-19 lockdown, mathematics scores rose by 8–11 points on a 100-point scale. Online learning worked better for reasoning-focused courses, such as mathematics, than for interpretation-focused courses, such as English.

Nonlinear pricing in multidimensional context: An empirical analysis of energy consumption

International Journal of Industrial Organization · 2023

Xintong Han, Zimin Liu & Tong Wang

Under combined block and time-of-use electricity prices, model simulations show that a 1% price increase reduces household electricity demand by about 0.7%. The analysis also finds limited room to improve such multidimensional tariffs.

Peer recognition, badge policies, and content contribution: An empirical study

Journal of Economic Behavior & Organization · 2023

Xintong Han, Yushen Li & Tong Wang

On Zhihu, a leading Chinese Q&A platform, upvotes from peers lead influencers to write more answers. Badges that certify identity or expertise can instead hold back contributions, because they raise reputation and privacy concerns.
