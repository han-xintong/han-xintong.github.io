Xintong Han is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Marketing at the Faculty of Business Administration, Université Laval. His research connects quantitative marketing, platform economics, and empirical industrial organization.

Before joining Université Laval in 2023, he was an Assistant Professor of Economics at Concordia University. He received his Ph.D. in Economics from the Toulouse School of Economics.

Research agenda

I ask how digital rules, pricing policies, and network structure shape user behavior, firm strategy, market outcomes, and welfare.

What I study

Four connected questions.

01

Digital platforms

How commission rules, badges, recommendation systems, and public platforms reshape participation, pricing, and quality.

02

Networks

How social learning, peer effects, and technological dependencies transmit information and adoption decisions across connected markets.

03

Pricing & welfare

How multidimensional price schedules change consumer demand, firm strategy, market allocation, and policy trade-offs.

04

Empirical methods

Structural econometrics, causal inference, and machine learning in econometrics using experiments, panel designs, instruments, and dynamic models.
